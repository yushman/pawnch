## The character and board art — Craftpix File License

Every human figure in this game — the two duel characters, the twelve board pieces baked into
`assets/board/pieces.png`, the graveyard figures, the margin commanders — is derived from free
Craftpix chibi packs. The rig parts live in `assets/rig/valkyrie/` and
`assets/rig/skeleton_warrior/`; `tools/import_craftpix.py` is what produces them from the pack
`.zip`, and `tools/bake_board_atlas.py` is what bakes the board atlas from the result.

Licence: <https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/>, read in full on 2026-08-17 and again on
2026-08-19 when eight more packs were adopted. What it was read for, and what it cost this
project, is in [ADR-0007](docs/adr/0007-identity-in-crest-and-weapon-on-a-shared-body.md).

Bodies, heads, faces and animations:

- *Free Chibi Skeleton Warrior Character Sprites* — the black side

Weapons, one per piece type, each a drop-in 400×128 part from a different free pack:

### What the licence permits

Clause 2.1: the assets may be used in any number of personal and commercial projects, modified
to requirements, included in game projects, and games containing them may be sold and
distributed. No attribution is required — this file exists anyway, because a reader should not
have to guess. So: shipping this art inside the APK, cutting it into parts, re-rigging it,
rasterising the pack's vector sources and recolouring any of it are all allowed.

### What it forbids

Clause 2.2: the art **source files** may not be resold, and may not be redistributed in a manner
that would make some or all of the art files usable to another end user via the app. Shipping
them as a packed atlas inside a game is not that; publishing them as a downloadable asset pack
would be.

### Clause 3.1.1 — the rule, not a footnote

> The Licensed Assets, "whether provided as free or premium content, may not be used … for the
> purposes of training, fine-tuning, developing, testing, validating, or improving any artificial
> intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML) … or similar systems."

**No Craftpix-derived art in this repository may ever be given to an image model, for any
purpose, in any role.** Not as training data, not as a style reference, not as a `--ref` or
`--sref` argument, not pasted into a chat to ask what it looks like. This applies to free and
premium Craftpix content alike; it is not a free-tier restriction. It applies to anything
derived from that art too — the rig parts, the weapons, `assets/board/pieces.png`, and any
screenshot or recording in `docs/media/` that shows the figures.

This is stated here in full because it has already changed the design of this project once. The
plan on Day 2 was to generate more character heads with a pack head as the style reference;
reading this clause killed that plan, and identity moved onto a procedurally drawn crest with no
pack lineage instead — [ADR-0007](docs/adr/0007-identity-in-crest-and-weapon-on-a-shared-body.md)
decisions 7 and 8. If you are about to feed a sprite from this repository to a generator, this
paragraph is the one that says do not.

The crests (`assets/rig/crest_*.png`, from `tools/crests.py`), the impact burst
(`assets/rig/burst.png`) and the sound effects (`assets/sfx/*.wav`, from `tools/sfx.py`) are
generated by code in this repository, have no pack lineage, and clause 3.1.1 does not reach them.
