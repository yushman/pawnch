## Implementation Tasks

Day 7 of `docs/mvp-plan.md` — the other four pieces, and the clash that guarantees nothing is
ever silent. Two half-days: **the weapon half of ADR-0007's identity**, and **the shared strike**.

### The plan is wrong about half of half-day 1, in our favour

The plan reads "remaining 4 piece types as crest **and weapon** swaps". The crests for all six
types already exist, are already generated by `tools/crests.py`, are already mapped in
`CharacterRig.CRESTS`, are already swapped by `set_piece_type()` and are already drawn on the
board atlas. That landed on Day 4.

**The weapon is the half that does not exist.** `%Sword` is one texture per rig and
`set_piece_type()` never touches it. ADR-0007 says identity lives in a crest *and* a weapon; only
one of those two channels is real today. So half-day 1 is the weapon, plus finally running the
silhouette test on crests that have shipped for three days without a person ever looking at them.

The freed time goes to the strike, not to the day finishing early — see below.

### The strike is not a placeholder; it is the clash the player sees most

Day 6's playtest: one full game against the bot produced **24 clashes — 19 charge, 5 pounce**, and
7 of the 19 were the queen alone. Rook, bishop, queen and king together are the overwhelming
majority of captures in a real game, and after this change they all resolve to the strike. Build
it as the most-watched animation in the game, because that is what it is. It does not have to beat
the two bespoke clashes; it has to survive being watched twenty times in one session.

### Decisions taken before this change was written

- **The six weapons come out of the Craftpix packs, not out of a drawing script.** Ten free chibi
  packs are already downloaded in `~/Downloads` and **every one of them puts its weapon on an
  identical 400×128 canvas with `pivot_x="0" pivot_y="1"`** — verified across all ten `.scml`
  files. A donor weapon is therefore a *pure texture drop-in* on the existing `Sword` sprite: no
  offset math, no grip anchor to measure, no per-type rig adjustment. This satisfies ADR-0009
  (the step ends in a script — extraction, rasterisation and mounting are all
  `tools/import_craftpix.py`'s job) and it is ADR-0007 decision 7's option (b) — "source more from
  other Craftpix freebie packs" — applied to weapons instead of heads, where it works because
  weapons *do* differ in outline and heads did not.
- **Proposed mapping, to be confirmed by the legibility check in block B**, not by taste:

  | type | weapon | pack |
  |---|---|---|
  | pawn | knobbly club | `zombie-villager` |
  | knight | thin spear / lance | `chibi-necromancer-of-the-shadow` |
  | bishop | staff with a round orb | `seer` |
  | rook | broad heavy blade | `chibi-skeleton-crusader` |
  | queen | staff with a flame head | `chibi-dark-oracle` |
  | king | cruciform sword | `chibi-valkyrie` |

  Unused and available if a pair fails: the skeleton warrior's falchion, the fallen angel's
  katana, the reaper's crook. **The bloody alchemist's cleaver is excluded on content grounds** —
  the blade carries blood, and this is a game for 6–12 year olds. The forest ranger's bow is
  excluded on mechanics: it is a 200×320 part on a different canvas, held differently, and it is
  the one donor that is not a drop-in.
- **The same weapon shape serves both sides**, as the crest does. A child learns one mapping. The
  two sides differ only in the raster size the shared source is rendered at.
- **The strike is an overhead smash.** The attacker barely crosses the floor: it winds up, raises
  high with a back-lean, drives a short lunge and slams down vertically. **On impact the defender
  is knocked backwards and settles** — the only clash that moves the defender, and the cheapest
  strong difference available given the rigs have five animations and nobody draws (ADR-0009).
  The charge is horizontal travel; the pounce leaves the ground; the strike is vertical energy
  delivered from where the attacker stands.
- **Target layout: 1.70 s end to end, impact at ~0.52 s.** A third distinct length against the
  charge's 1.90 s and the pounce's 1.80 s — the suite already asserts that two clashes of one
  length read as one clash, and three is no different. Shorter than both on purpose: it is seen
  19 times a session and it is the one that must not outstay its welcome.
- **`FALLBACK_CLASH` moves from `"charge"` to `"strike"`.** `CLASH_BY_TYPE` gains
  `bishop`, `rook`, `king` → `"strike"`. The queen is *deliberately left out of the table* and
  takes the fallback, which lands it on the strike anyway, until Day 8 gives it its own clash. The
  name `"strike"` was freed for exactly this on Day 6 when Day 3's clash was renamed `charge`.
- **A third skip sweep, not a stated reason to skip it.** The strike is the first clash to move
  the defender, so it is the first clash whose skip can leave the *defender* somewhere wrong. That
  is new failure surface, and the sweep is how it is covered. Cost is ~4 s of suite time.
- **The legibility check is done with a real person, today.** The crests have shipped for three
  days on the strength of nobody having looked at them, and there is now someone who has played a
  full game and can be asked. Block B produces the sheet; the verdict comes back to the main
  session and decides whether cut order step 4 fires.

### Traps found in the code before starting — do not rediscover these

- **`scenes/character_rig_*.tscn` are generated and must never be hand-edited** (ADR-0009). Weapon
  work changes `tools/import_craftpix.py` and re-runs it, for both packs.
- **Ghostscript is now installed** (`/opt/homebrew/bin/gs`), so donor weapons rasterise from `EPS/`
  at the family's own raster factor — valkyrie `1.75` → 700×224, skeleton `2.0` → 800×256, which
  is exactly the size each family's existing `sword.png` already is. Do **not** upscale the packs'
  400×128 pre-rendered PNGs.
- **`tools/verify_rig.py` compares the rig against the pack's own rendered frames, and `Slashing`
  shows the sword.** Swapping the weapon by default would break that comparison silently. Follow
  the `show_crest` / `show_idle_weapon` precedent: an exported flag on `CharacterRig` that
  `tests/render_rig_frames.gd` turns off so verification keeps seeing the pack's own sword.
- **`_warm()` warms nothing this change adds.** Six new textures per side and the strike's own
  transform path are first-use costs that would otherwise land on the player's first capture —
  which is the window failure mode 4 is about.
- **Widen the guard before you write the thing it guards.** `_reset_stage()` already restores
  position for both figures, so the knockback is covered — but confirm it, in that order, before
  writing the knockback. That ordering is what caught the pounce's draw-order bug.
- **Images placed anywhere under the project directory get imported by Godot and shipped in the
  APK.** Any new reference or contact-sheet directory needs a `.gdignore`, as `docs/media/` has.
- **`godot --headless --import` after adding textures**, or `load()` returns a stale texture from
  `.godot/imported/`.
- **`MIN_CHECKS = 184` in `tests/run_headless.gd` is a floor that must be raised with the suite**,
  or the new checks can silently stop running.
- **Every clash already drops exactly one vsync** — 1 frame in 108 against an idle baseline of 1 in
  ~892, worst gap 29.5 ms for the charge and 53–58 ms for the pounce against a 20 ms budget. It
  reproduces warm, it is a panel cost, it is stated in both READMEs and **it is Day 9's perf pass,
  not this change's**. Do not chase it; do not make it worse. The strike's frame-timing ritual is
  deferred with the other two, and this change does not claim the strike passes it.
- **Do not add a second way to apply a move**, and do not improve the bot (ADR-0005).
- `adb exec-out screencap -p` returns a stale frame on this device; anything moving must be
  captured with `adb shell screenrecord` and split with `ffmpeg -vf fps=N`.
- Board taps are computable: `x = (rank-1)*135 + 67.5`, `y = 520 + file_index*170 + 85`. The debug
  Duel button at `956 2336` plays the **charge**, hardcoded to knight-takes-pawn.
